Vibe Tracks home shell

Round 3: Review is Zen mode, media first — one decision at a time, triaged, options shaped like Claude’s questions, and comments pinned on the media.

Built from Zach’s “Feedback on review mode” (Daily Note, ~line 1257) on top of the round-2 shell; Zen’s structure and keys are ported from the Oct 4 “Needs you” N1/N6 lane. Still a clickable mock: answers leave through one Copy, nothing is sent.
  • No inbox. Review opens on the first decision; triaged order is blocking now → waiting for you (no default) → defaulting without you (optional).
  • Media leads every card — a looping video, a before/after screenshot, a KPI chart or a diff — and the text supports it. Every figure is labelled real · source or illustrative.
  • Options like Claude’s AskUserQuestion: numbered, the recommended one first with “(Recommended)”, then “4. Other”. ↵ takes the recommendation, 1–3 pick, N adds a note, M pins a comment on a spot of the image / chart / diff or on the paused video frame (with its timestamp), S skips.
  • Richer top bar: ids gone; each step shows its state — ✓ recommendation · ✎ different or commented · ↷ skipped (default applies, or still waiting when there is none) · ◌ defaulting unseen · ● current — with its title in the tooltip.
  • Entry points: Review in the sidebar with a count badge, “Needs you” on a track row (opens at that track’s first decision), and the track page’s Review tab (filtered to that track). The end is a calm summary: decided · defaulting · still waiting · back to the agents.
  • Also fixed: Board card sparklines now sit at one offset in every card, and the agent count never wraps (measured).

Your Oct 6 feedback, built into all five

These live in the shared track and project pages, so every variant has them; the variants still differ in shell, home, board and where things surface. Daily Note lines ~785-870.

  • Many KPIs, one summary. The row sparkline is the track's summary statistic (its first KPI); the track page shows every soft KPI as small multiples side by side so a Goodhart trade-off is visible, and hard gates sit apart as pass / fail (evo: optimisation with hard and soft constraints). A failing gate is what stalls Hyperspectral (“data drive mounted: fail”).
  • Done and Archived. Every board has both, and you can drag a card into them (or back to On track); Needs you / Stalled refuse a drop because they are computed. Rows hide archived behind “Show archived”.
  • North star = lagging, tracks = leading. The project page draws the north star on top and each track's KPI under it on the same time axis, with a guide at every north-star step, so “tracks moved first” is visible.
  • Auditor. A tab per track: each LLM-judge round with model, verdict, findings marked open / fixed, time to verdict and a link to its audit file; rows and cards carry a badge like “r3 pass·fix · 1 open”.
  • Discover vs Optimize. A mode switch on every track; a track with no KPIs sits in Discover, where the agent's proposed KPIs and gates wait for Accept / Reject, and Optimize unlocks after the first accepted KPI.
  • Suggested skills (not built): /vibetracks, /vibetracks-loop (creates KPIs first if there are none) and /vibetracks-discover as one-click actions on the track page; they show the session they would start.
